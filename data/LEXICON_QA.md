## Required before adding or promoting entries

1. Confirm each form with a Chuukese or Unanu/Namonuito speaker, trusted dictionary, or community-provided source.
2. Store language and variant separately; never silently merge Chuukese and Namonuito forms.
3. Preserve spelling, pronunciation, English gloss, source, and reviewer identity independently.
4. Mark machine-extracted or unconfirmed forms unverified.
5. Mark conflicting, contaminated, or unclear forms quarantine.
6. Promote to reviewed or approved only after community or expert review.
7. Keep a source URL and an auditable provenance note for every record.
